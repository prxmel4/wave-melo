# wavemelo v0.5.0

## Highlights

- Added `/seek` with strict timestamp parsing and safe validation against
  authoritative Lavalink track metadata.
- Added `/nowplaying` with a full playback summary and a progress bar that is
  safe for live streams and unknown durations.

## Added

- `src/playback/seek.rs`: pure parser for `90`, `01:30`, and `00:01:30`, with
  checked arithmetic, rejection of signs/Unicode digits/malformed components,
  and track-metadata validation.
- `src/ui/now_playing.rs`: owned view data and embed rendering with title, URL,
  artist, requester, position, duration, volume, loop, filter, and status.
- Slash commands `/seek` and `/nowplaying`.

## Changed

- Seek authority comes from `TrackInfo` (`is_seekable`, `is_stream`, `length`)
  and the position returned by Lavalink after `set_position`.

## Upgrade instructions

Restart the bot to register the two new slash commands. No configuration or
Lavalink changes are required.

## Known limitations

- Seeking exactly to or beyond the track end is rejected rather than clamped.
- Live Discord interactions and audio output still require a live deployment.
